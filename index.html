    How to read the wait values

    • Minutes / hours — estimated wait for non-urgent patients, published by the clinic.
    • Busyness 0–10 — White Cross clinics publish a busyness level, not minutes (0 = quiet, 10 = very busy).
    • Greyed out — data older than 30 minutes or the source could not be reached; do not rely on it.
    • No live data — please phone the clinic before going.
